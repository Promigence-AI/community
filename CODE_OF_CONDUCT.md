# Code of conduct

Be kind.

This community exists to help people run their agents on Promigence and to make the product better, so keep threads on that and assume good intent. Argue about ideas and leave people out of it.

Criticism of the product is welcome here, and the more specific it is, the faster it gets fixed. There is no room for harassment, spam, unrelated self-promotion or illegal content, and nobody may post secrets, API keys or anyone's private information.

Moderators may edit, hide or remove posts that break these rules, lock a thread that has run its course and block anyone who keeps at it. If something needs our attention, email support@promigence.ai.
